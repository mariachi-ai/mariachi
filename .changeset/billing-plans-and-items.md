Subscriptions with several prices: `subscribe`, `changePlan` and `createCheckout` accept `items: [{ priceId, quantity? }]` instead of `priceId` (exactly one of the two), a quantity is only sent when given (Stripe rejects one on a metered price), and `changePlan` with `items` replaces the item set by price. `Subscription.items` lists every item (`planId` and `quantity` stay the first item's), and `hasActiveSubscription` matches any item's price. Plans by lookup key: `Plan.lookupKey` and `Plan.productMetadata`, `findPlanByLookupKey(ctx, key)`, and `listPlans(ctx, { lookupKeys })`; price webhooks keep the product fields the mirror has. Allowance resets: `setCreditBalance(ctx, target, options)` appends the one ledger entry that reaches `target` under the ledger lock. The ledger's `currency` is documented as any 3-letter unit code. Dimensions: ledger entries and usage records take optional `dimensions`, summed per key by `summarizeCredits` and `getUsageBreakdown`. `StripeAdapter.listPlans({ activeOnly: false })` now lists every price rather than only inactive ones. `BillingStore` gains `findPlan`, `setCreditLedgerBalance`, `summarizeCreditsByDimension` and `summarizeUsageByDimension`, and `BillingAdapter.listPlans` takes `lookupKeys`.

New columns: `billing_subscriptions.items`, `billing_plans.lookup_key` (indexed) and `billing_plans.product_metadata`, and `dimensions` on `billing_credit_transactions` and `billing_usage_records`. Run `mariachi db generate` to create the migration. All are nullable; subscriptions mirrored before the upgrade read `items: []` until their next webhook or sync.
